In SFTTrainer (Supervised Fine-Tuning Trainer) — like the one used in Hugging Face’s trl or other transformer fine-tuning frameworks — the packing parameter controls how input sequences are combined to maximize GPU utilization during training.


Let’s say the model can take 1000 words at a time.
And you have 3 samples:
Sample 1 → 300 words
Sample 2 → 200 words
Sample 3 → 400 words

If packing = False,
each sample is used alone and filled with empty space (padding) like this:


[Sample 1 + 700 empty]
[Sample 2 + 800 empty]
[Sample 3 + 600 empty]

That wastes space and time.

If packing = True,
the trainer joins them together:

[Sample 1 + Sample 2 + Sample 3] = 900 words


Now it fits in one batch — no space wasted, training is faster.
